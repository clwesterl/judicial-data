judicial-data

Interactive pages built from the Supreme Court Database, the Judicial Common Space, and the Comprehensive Court of Appeals Database. Each one is a single self-contained file; the code that generates it lives in scdb-expert/scripts/.

Supreme Court

  • Median of the Majority OT1937–2022 The justice who most often held the median position inside the winning coalition each term, plotted at their JCS score against the Court median.
  • Majority coalitions OT1946–2025 Every set of justices that formed a majority, as a drill-down graph: chief justice to natural court to term, sized by case count.
  • Coalitions by majority ideology OT1946–2022 The same coalitions as a ranked dot plot, each placed at the median JCS score of its members, with the term-to-term spread of its per-case medians.

Courts of Appeals

  • First Circuit Seats 1891–2026 Every authorized judgeship on the First Circuit as a lane on a timeline, showing who held each seat and where they sat ideologically.
Source and data: github.com/clwesterl/judicial-data. The two coalition pages need an internet connection on first open (d3 from a CDN); the other two are fully self-contained apart from webfonts.
